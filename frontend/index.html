                What friends can see

                Tiny Town only shows what you choose to share, and never makes things up about you.

                • Friends see your name, @username, character and interests.
                • People in your towns also see the name and color you picked there, your house, and your character walking around.
                • Anything you share (like a good week or a rough one) is shown the way you pick: fully, vaguely, or not at all.
                • Nothing leaves the town (a message, an invite, a plan) unless a person approves it.
                • Nobody can find you by email, only by your username.

                Your data

                Download a copy of your profile, towns, friends and everything you've shared, as a JSON file.

                Delete account

                Permanently deletes your profile, friends, shared updates and characters. Towns you created pass to their longest-standing member; empty ones are deleted. This can't be undone.

                Help

                Tiny Town is a little isometric city where every character is one of your real friends. The town notices what's going on in friends' lives (only what they share) and suggests real-world ways to show up for each other.

                How do friends find me?

                By your @username. Share it with them, or search for theirs on the home page. Nobody can search by email.

                How do I join a town?

                A town's creator can invite any of their friends, or give out the town's invite code. When you join, you pick the name you'll go by there and your color.

                What does the town know about me?

                Only what you choose to share, shown fully, vaguely or not at all. See Privacy & data.

                Can the town message my friends for me?

                No. Characters can suggest things inside the town, but nothing reaches a real person unless a human approves it.

                How do I change my look, name or color?

                Your character's look is the same everywhere: change it in Your character. Your name and color are picked per town when you join, and you can change them in Towns. Nobody in the same town can share your name or a color too close to yours.

                How do I leave a town or delete my account?

                Both are in Privacy & data.
